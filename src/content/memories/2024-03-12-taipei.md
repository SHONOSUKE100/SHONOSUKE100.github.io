A 4-day, 3-night solo trip to Taipei.

I visited as many famous spots in Taipei as possible, truly immersing myself in its history and culture.

Since I focused so much on sightseeing, I didn't have much time to relax at cafes or enjoy leisurely meals, which was a bit of a regret.

While traveling alone let me move quickly from place to place, next time I'd like to go with someone else and enjoy a more relaxed pace.
